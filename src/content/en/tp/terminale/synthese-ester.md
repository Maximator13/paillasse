## Principle

3-methylbutyl ethanoate, which smells of banana, is obtained by the reaction between ethanoic acid and 3-methylbutan-1-ol:

$$
\mathrm{CH_3COOH + C_5H_{11}OH \rightleftarrows CH_3COOC_5H_{11} + H_2O}
$$

This esterification is **slow** and **limited**: it leads to an equilibrium state, with constant $K \approx 4$ for a primary alcohol. To speed it up, the mixture is heated and a catalyst (sulfuric acid) is added. **Heating under reflux** keeps the mixture boiling without any loss of material: the vapours condense in the condenser and fall back into the flask.

## Method

1. In the fume cupboard, place in the flask $21.8\ \mathrm{mL}$ of 3-methylbutan-1-ol ($0.20\ \mathrm{mol}$), $11.4\ \mathrm{mL}$ of ethanoic acid ($0.20\ \mathrm{mol}$), 5 drops of concentrated sulfuric acid and a few pumice granules.
2. Clamp the flask to the retort stand and fit the bulb condenser vertically. Connect the water: in at the bottom, out at the top.
3. Switch on the heating mantle and keep the mixture boiling gently for 60 min. The vapours must not rise above the first third of the condenser.
4. Observe the curve of the extent of reaction: it levels off when equilibrium is reached.
5. Switch off the heating, lower the heating mantle and leave to cool without stopping the water flow. Calculate the yield.

## Analysis

The mixture is equimolar: $x_\mathrm{max} = 0.20\ \mathrm{mol}$. At equilibrium, the reaction quotient is equal to $K$ (water is not the solvent, so it appears in $Q_r$):

$$
Q_{r,\mathrm{eq}} = \frac{x_f^2}{(0.20 - x_f)^2} = 4
$$

It follows that:

$$
x_f = \frac{2}{3} \times 0.20 = 0.133\ \mathrm{mol}
$$

The yield is therefore:

$$
\eta = \frac{x_f}{x_\mathrm{max}} = \frac{0.133}{0.20} = 67\ \%
$$

which gives a mass of ester $m = 0.133 \times 130.2 = 17.4\ \mathrm{g}$, instead of $26.0\ \mathrm{g}$ for a complete reaction.

| Factor | Rate | Yield at equilibrium |
|---|---|---|
| Higher temperature | increases | unchanged |
| Catalyst | increases | unchanged |
| Reactant in excess | increases | increases |

The yield at equilibrium is still 67%: temperature and catalyst are kinetic factors, they do not change the final state. At 60 °C, the mixture no longer boils: it is the boiling sustained by the reflux that allows strong heating, and therefore saves time.

## Safety

Concentrated sulfuric acid and pure ethanoic acid are corrosive, and the alcohol is flammable: wear a lab coat, safety glasses and gloves, and work in a fume cupboard. Never stopper the top of the condenser: the apparatus must stay open to the air to avoid any build-up of pressure.
